Native macOS menu bar audio mixer with signed Sparkle updates. Requires macOS 14.2 or newer and an Apple silicon Mac.

**New in this version**
- **Smart mode:** right-click the menu bar icon and choose **Smart Mode** for a simpler panel laid out like Control Centre’s Sound: the volume, a slim slider for each app that’s playing, and your outputs. Your microphone follows the output, so a headset brings its own microphone and the Mac’s speakers bring the Mac’s.
- The panel is now one layer of Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later. Before, two layers made it look grey.
- The menu bar icon is the same size as Control Centre’s Sound icon.
- Headsets such as the Jabra Evolve2 show a headphones icon in the device list.
